# The Mechanism Signature

### The registry scored for a moment

Scored for a particular moment, the registry becomes a **mechanism signature**: each entry carries an intensity from 0 to 100, a direction, and, where the mechanism is absent, a written reason for the zero. The scores describe assessed presence and load, not probability.

That vector is the working object. It carries historical comparison, analogue retrieval, negative-space analysis, dated public readings, and conditioning of a language model with a short structured state instead of a long narrative.

## How signatures are kept

A signature is a dated reading, and the discipline around it is part of the object.

1. A signature declares the vocabulary version it was read against. Signatures written against different versions are not compared without a bridge.
2. Zero carries a written reason. An unreasoned "absent" produces an archive that agrees with itself.
3. Direction is required. A rising 12 and a falling 12 are the same number and different worlds.
4. Every signature carries a cut-off date, a review date, and at least three refutation conditions.
5. A signature is sealed. New information arrives as a new dated signature, not as an edit.
6. If the refuters hold, the signature is withdrawn whole rather than repaired line by line.

## Scope

The signature layer runs on Claude Opus. Signatures, their calibration and the runs themselves stay in the research system; this repository publishes the vocabulary they are written in.
