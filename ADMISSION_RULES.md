# Admission Rules

The number of mechanisms is not a target. Expansion occurs only when evidence forces a representational change.

## Public admission gate

A candidate must satisfy all of the following. Criteria 2, 3 and 5 are the gates a candidate is filtered on; the rest are requirements on the entry and on how it is introduced.

1. **Causal distinctness**  
   It describes a process with a defensible causal direction, not merely a theme, actor, asset, symptom, or outcome.

2. **Irreducibility**  
   Existing mechanisms, alone or in combination, cannot represent the candidate without losing a material causal distinction.

3. **Recurrence**  
   The structure appears across independent cases rather than as a single event's vocabulary.

4. **Operational boundary**  
   Reviewers can state when the mechanism is present, absent, unknown, or better represented by a neighbouring identifier.

5. **Comparative value**  
   Adding it improves cross-case retrieval, explanation, phase coding, or falsifiability enough to justify migration cost.

6. **Adversarial survival**  
   The proposal addresses its nearest counterargument: duplication, excessive breadth, excessive specificity, or dependence on one narrative.

7. **Version-safe introduction**  
   The entry receives a stable identifier, bounded definition, default phase role, `introduced_in` value, and changelog record.

## What does not qualify

- a new label for an existing mechanism;
- a mechanism inferred from one vivid case only;
- a measured indicator or threshold;
- a sector, country, institution, or asset name;
- a bundle whose components are already represented without causal loss;
- a useful case-specific phrase that does not need to become canonical.

## Status of a refusal

A refusal is a dated judgment against the registry as it stood, not a verdict on the phenomenon. The ground of refusal states what would overturn it:

| Ground | What reverses it |
|---|---|
| **Reducible** — existing entries, alone or in combination, already carry it | A case where the combination holds but the distinction it is said to carry does not, or the reverse |
| **Non-recurring** — drawn from a single case | Further independent cases |
| **Not a mechanism** — an indicator, an outcome, an actor, or a topic | Nothing. The category is wrong, and evidence does not correct a category |

`twin_crisis` and `sudden_stop` were refused on the first ground, and that ground remains open. `fiscal_dominance` was admitted through it: the candidate read as `sovereign_funding_stress` combined with `policy_repricing` until the pair was shown to encode symptoms without direction.

## Decision record

Public releases document admitted mechanisms and their rationale. The working candidate ledger remains private because it contains incomplete hypotheses, duplicated concepts, internal deliberation, and unreviewed evidence.

Rejected candidates may be discussed publicly when doing so improves the boundary of an existing mechanism.
