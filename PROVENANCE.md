## How this registry was formed

The public registry grew from repeated coding of hundreds of historical crises and near-crises. Candidate mechanisms were compared across cases, merged when they duplicated an existing structure, narrowed when their boundaries were too loose, and promoted only when the existing vocabulary could not represent the recurring causal process without distortion.

The public files are derived from that work, but they do not expose the private corpus, source graph, case files, candidate notebook, calibration process, automation, or live mechanism signatures.

## Public record

Each public release fixes the mechanism identifiers, definitions, boundaries, and default phase roles used at that point in the research. Later changes belong to later versions. This keeps citations and experiments attached to the vocabulary that actually generated them.

The repository presentation and its validation tooling were developed with OpenAI Codex.
