# Contributing Criticism and Proposals

The most useful contributions are precise challenges to the registry's boundaries.

## Welcome

- a prior-art reference that materially overlaps the registry or method;
- a historical counterexample;
- evidence that two mechanisms are duplicates;
- evidence that one mechanism is too broad or too narrow;
- a candidate mechanism with a causal chain and repeated cases;
- a translation or machine-readable consistency correction.

## Candidate proposal format

1. Proposed stable identifier
2. One-sentence causal definition
3. Causal chain
4. Nearest existing mechanism or combination
5. What material distinction is lost without the candidate
6. Independent historical cases
7. Counterexample or exclusion boundary
8. Proposed default phase role

A proposal does not become canonical merely by being submitted. Admission follows [ADMISSION_RULES.md](ADMISSION_RULES.md) and a versioned release decision.

Do not submit confidential, personal, proprietary, or unlawfully obtained material.
