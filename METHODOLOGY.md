# Methodology

## Unit of representation

The registry's unit is a **causal mechanism**: a recurring process that links conditions, actions, and consequences.

A mechanism is not:

- an indicator (`inflation = 4.7%`);
- an event (`the 2008 crisis`);
- an asset class (`bonds`);
- a topic (`geopolitics`);
- a forecast (`recession next quarter`);
- a case-specific phrase that cannot travel across cases.

Mechanism judgments may be supported by indicators and sources, but the identifier represents the causal process rather than its measurement.

## Construction process

The registry is built through repeated historical coding rather than through one-off brainstorming.

1. **Read a case as a causal sequence.** Separate preconditions, trigger, amplification, transmission, policy response, resolution, and residue.
2. **Code with the existing registry first.** New language is not promoted merely because a case is unusual.
3. **Preserve residual mechanics outside the registry.** Case-specific detail remains available without fragmenting the canonical vocabulary.
4. **Collect candidates longitudinally.** A candidate must recur and expose a stable representational gap.
5. **Test the nearest existing mechanisms.** If a combination of existing mechanisms preserves the same causal direction and analytical use, no new identifier is needed.
6. **Define a boundary.** Every admitted entry must state what it includes and what nearby concept it does not replace.
7. **Freeze a public release.** Published identifiers and meanings remain reproducible.

## Closed vocabulary, living registry

These are not contradictory:

- **Closed within a version:** only identifiers present in that release are valid canonical values.
- **Living across versions:** evidence may justify additive entries in a later release.

The discipline prevents vocabulary drift without claiming that research is finished.

## Phase model

Each mechanism has one or more default phase-role priors:

| Phase | Question |
|---|---|
| `trigger` | What crossed the threshold or lit the fuse? |
| `amplification` | What multiplied loss or fragility within a node? |
| `transmission` | How did stress travel between institutions, markets, sectors, or borders? |
| `policy` | What did an authority do or credibly threaten to do? |
| `resolution` | What ended or contained the episode, and what new fragility did it leave? |

Default roles are priors, not claims about every occurrence. A mechanism may play a different role in a specific event when the evidence supports an override.

## Downstream mechanism signatures

The registry can be used to construct a vector of mechanism intensities. In that downstream representation:

- scores represent assessed presence and load, not probability;
- `0` means observed absence only when absence was assessed;
- unknown or unassessed is not encoded as zero;
- every signature cites the registry version it uses.

This release documents the registry, not the calibration recipe or any live signature.

## Epistemic limits

The registry does not eliminate judgment. It makes part of that judgment inspectable and repeatable.

- A mechanism assignment can be wrong.
- Two mechanisms can co-exist without one causing the other.
- Historical similarity does not establish prediction.
- The registry is finance-first and may underrepresent mechanisms from other domains.
- Default phase roles can hide case-specific role changes.

These are the reasons for versioning, counterexamples, and adversarial review.
